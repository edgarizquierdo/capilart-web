Quick answer

How do you choose scalp micropigmentation in Barcelona?

There is no single “best” treatment for every case. The choice depends on whether you are looking for density between existing hairs, a efecto rapado, reinforce the coronilla, camouflage a cicatriz or visually complement the result of a hair transplant. Before deciding, it is advisable to review cases comparable to yours, assess the design individually and understand what the technique can achieve and what its limitations are.

Before choosing

What to look for in a scalp micropigmentation specialist

1. Real cases similar to yours

It is not enough to see one good isolated result. If you are looking for density with long hair, crown treatment, scar camouflage or a shaved effect, review examples of the same type of work.

2. An individual assessment before discussing design

Skin tone, hair colour and quantity, hair-loss pattern, scalp condition and visual goal all affect how treatment should be planned.

3. Realistic expectations

Micropigmentation is a visual aesthetic technique. It does not make hair grow, stop alopecia or remove a scar. Its aim is to reduce contrast and visually recreate the appearance of hair follicles.

4. Progressive treatment

Skin does not retain pigment in the same way in every person or every area. That is why the result is built progressively and adjusted according to how it evolves.

5. Integration, not saturation

A natural result depends on adapting size, tone, distribution and intensity to the existing hair. More pigment does not necessarily mean a better result.

6. Follow-up and aftercare

Pre-treatment information, aftercare and reviewing the healing process are part of the treatment, especially in skin types or areas with irregular retention.

Questions people often ask before deciding

Price, sessions and duration

How much does scalp micropigmentation cost in Barcelona?

The price mainly depends on the size of the area, type of work and number of sessions needed. A reliable quote requires assessing the case first. You can see how it is calculated on the pricing and assessment page.

How many sessions are needed?

Treatment is built over several sessions. The exact number depends on the size of the area, skin type, pigment retention and each person's goal.

How long does it last?

Intensity gradually decreases over time. Skin, sun exposure, habits and individual response influence how it evolves and when maintenance may be appropriate.

Can it be assessed remotely?

Yes. Many cases can initially be assessed using clear photographs and, when there is a good amount of hair, a video parting it to show the scalp.
