Scalp micropigmentation prices in Barcelona

I do not use one fixed price for every case because the treatment area, goal and complexity can vary greatly. The assessment is used to define which areas should be treated and prepare a clear quote for your case.

What does the price depend on?

Area size

An isolated crown does not require the same amount of work as the entire top area or a full shaved effect.

Existing density

The actual contrast between skin and hair determines how much integration work is needed.

Type of treatment

Density work, shaved effect, scars and post-transplant cases require different approaches.

Sessions

Treatment is built progressively and the final number of sessions depends on how each person's skin evolves.

Why I prefer to assess before giving a price

Advertising a very low “from” price may be commercially attractive, but it does not help someone who needs to know what their own case will really cost. My aim is to assess the extent first and propose a coherent treatment plan so you know what is being recommended and why.

Does the assessment commit me to treatment?

No. The assessment is used to determine whether micropigmentation can provide a visual improvement, explain the proposed approach and answer questions before you make a decision.

Price and quality of the result

In scalp micropigmentation, technical judgement, natural appearance and planning are particularly important because the pigment remains visible on the scalp. Comparing only on price can overlook aspects such as design, integration, a conservative first session and follow-up.

What a well-planned treatment includes

The quote does not depend only on “how many centimetres” need to be pigmented. Technical difficulty changes depending on whether the case involves density between hairs, a full shaved effect, a crown, a scar or integration of a previous transplant. The size of the area and the sessions needed to build the result safely and progressively also matter.

Why do I not use one price for everyone?

Two people may point to the same area and need very different approaches. One may still have plenty of hair and need only light integration; another may have extensive loss requiring treatment of several areas to avoid visible differences. Giving a fixed figure without seeing the case can create the wrong expectations.

How to receive a personalised quote

The quickest way is to send well-lit photos from the front, sides, top, crown and back. When there is a good amount of hair, a video parting it allows a better assessment of the scalp. With that material I can explain the proposed approach, the areas worth treating and the corresponding quote.

Price should not be the only criterion

With micropigmentation, the result remains visible on the scalp for an extended period. In addition to cost, it is worth considering the natural appearance of the work, specific SMP experience, design judgement, hygiene, quality of follow-up and the professional's ability to say when a case is not suitable.
