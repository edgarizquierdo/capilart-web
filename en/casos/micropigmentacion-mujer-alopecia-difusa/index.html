Female scalp micropigmentation for diffuse thinning

Real female scalp micropigmentation case for diffuse alopecia and fine hair. Density effect on the top area completed in 3 sessions.

↔

Density effect · Woman · 3 sessions

The patient had fine hair and diffuse density loss across the top area. She also reported increased hair loss after pregnancy, without CapilArt making any diagnosis as to the cause.

Treatment was extended across the entire top area rather than focusing only on the most obvious thinning. The aim was to reduce contrast between the light hair and scalp and create a more even appearance.

The result was built progressively over three sessions, adjusting visual density to maintain integration with the existing hair.

Real CapilArt BCN case. Results are individual and depend on the starting point, skin, existing hair and how the pigment evolves.
