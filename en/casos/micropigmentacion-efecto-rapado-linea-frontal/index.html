Shaved-effect scalp micropigmentation and hairline

Real shaved-effect micropigmentation case with hairline reconstruction, lateral integration and a design tailored to facial features and age.

↔

Shaved effect · Hairline · 3 sessions

The patient had advanced alopecia in the frontal and top areas and retained hair mainly on the sides and back.

The top area was visually reconstructed using a shaved effect. The hairline was designed to respect facial proportions, age and natural recession, avoiding an excessively low or artificial line.

The treatment was completed over three sessions to progressively blend the micropigmentation with the naturally shaved hair on the sides and back.

Real CapilArt BCN case. Results are individual and depend on the starting point, skin, existing hair and how the pigment evolves.
