Real experience

Scalp micropigmentation before and after in Barcelona

Female and male density work, crowns, long hair, shaved effects, congenital triangular alopecia and scar camouflage. Every case starts from a different situation and requires adapting the design, intensity, treatment area and number of sessions.

There is no single micropigmentation approach that suits everyone

These comparisons show why individual assessment is essential. Micropigmentation does not create hair: it changes the visual perception of the scalp and must blend with the existing hair, skin tone and each person's goal.
