Information about the use of cookies and similar technologies on this website.

1. Current website status

The current version of capilartbcn.com does not implement first-party analytics, behavioural advertising or tracking tools such as Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or equivalents. CapilArt BCN therefore does not request consent through a banner for non-essential cookies that it does not currently use.

2. Technical cookies

Where necessary, the site may use cookies or technical mechanisms that are essential for basic functions, security or navigation. Strictly necessary cookies are exempt from the requirement to obtain consent, without prejudice to the duty to inform.

3. Third-party services and links

The contact page loads third-party technical resources for the form to operate, including EmailJS through a content delivery network. The website also contains links to external services such as WhatsApp and Instagram. When following those links or using third-party services, those providers may apply their own cookies or technologies in accordance with their policies, outside CapilArt BCN's direct control.

4. Browser controls

You can configure your browser to block or delete cookies. Blocking strictly technical cookies may affect the operation of certain sites or external services.

5. Future changes

If measurement, advertising or other technologies requiring consent are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated and, before activation, a mechanism will be implemented to allow users to validly accept or reject non-essential cookies.

6. Contact

For any enquiry related to this policy, you can write to capilartbcn@gmail.com.
